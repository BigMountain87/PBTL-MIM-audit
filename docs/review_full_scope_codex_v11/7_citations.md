## Must-Fix

- **[17], passages 1 and 3: one-shot auditing is not trust-region model management.** Expensive evaluations in Alexandrov’s algorithm inform an iterative optimization procedure. The paper does not supply the claimed frozen-model, certification-only protocol, and its guarantees cannot be transferred by calling that protocol a “minimal form.”

- **[56], passage 6: boundary proximity and boundary violation are different conditions.** The manuscript defines T2 as any normalized parameter within 0.05 of 0 or 1. An interior point such as \(u=0.02\) triggers T2 but incurs no outside-box boundary penalty. Therefore “exactly the excursion” and “9–18 … would have been pushed back” do not follow.

- **[49], passage 5: calibration is misrepresented as distribution-shift correction.** Guo’s classification calibration results do not establish reliable uncertainty for shifted inverse-design inputs. Rewrite the shared predicate “designed for exactly this situation.”

- **[15]: correct the Design-Bench venue.**

## Should-Fix

- **[19,20] are fair review citations.** “Refined that bargain” is acceptable as broad historical synthesis. The problem is the preceding universal “only if” and certification framing, not citing reviews for developments in model management.

- **[21–23]: distinguish literature coverage from the manuscript’s interpretation.** These works support multifidelity modeling; they do not collectively establish that transferred weights reproduce autoregressive/composite modeling or that every multifidelity method provides terminal certification.

- **[13,24,25]: replace the blanket characterization of remedies.** Adapting a candidate generator, retraining a generative representation, and adapting a predictive oracle are different operations. Weighted retraining also should not be presented without qualification as strictly offline optimization.

- **Best-of-n: cite [30] explicitly in that sentence.** Gao directly studies best-of-n. [28] supplies the Goodhart taxonomy; [29] supplies formal reward-hacking analysis. Neither should substitute for the direct best-of-n source. Use “can degrade,” since proxy selection does not necessarily worsen true quality at every increase in selection budget.

- **[32]: verify optimizer-directed acquisition.** Active learning of a surrogate is not automatically sampling along the current optimization trajectory.

- **[38]: preserve the domain restriction.** Its empirical scope is fluid-related PDEs.

- **[47–51]: qualify the collective UQ claim.** Predictive uncertainty, confidence calibration, and robustness under shift are related but distinct. Ovadia reports empirical behavior across tested shifts, not a theorem that every method always degrades.

- **Passage 5’s uncited follow-ons also need qualification.** Optimizer-selected inputs are not outside the training distribution “by construction.” Likewise, an ordinary conformal wrapper does not automatically provide coverage for adaptively selected designs; its assumptions must be addressed.

- **[56]: distinguish evaluation from commitment.** Resimulating candidates to choose a winner and auditing a previously committed winner share a solver-evaluation operation but have different selection roles. Neither operation automatically entails computing the manuscript’s Δ statistic.

- **Passage 3’s investment/cost claims are the authors’ judgments.** “The better long-run investment” and “the only one … bounded by one call per design” are not established by the cited papers. State the one-call cost as a property of the proposed protocol.
